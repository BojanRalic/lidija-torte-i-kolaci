## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages. The client pays only for the domain (ideas: lidijatorteikolaci.rs, torteleskovac.rs). Speed matters. ## Users

- Private customers from Leskovac and the surrounding area (Vlasotince, Lebane, Bojnik, Grdelica, Niš, Vranje): parents planning a child's birthday, couples planning a wedding, hosts of a slava, families marking a christening, a birth, an 18th, 40th, 50th or 60th birthday.
- Diaspora: people living abroad who often order a cake for family in Leskovac.
- Occasionally cafés and restaurants (cups of cake, small cakes).
- Most arrive from Instagram (@lidijatorte, 3,515 followers, their best channel) or from Google Maps, on a phone.

Their job: find out whether Lidija can make the cake they have in mind, roughly what it costs and how early to order, then message or call.

## Product Purpose

A home page and site for "Lidija Torte i Kolači", a family cake workshop at Deligradska 11, Leskovac. Lidija Petković and her son Milan, with family, have made cakes and pastries for 20 years; registered as a business since 2020. Success: more Viber/WhatsApp messages and calls from people who already know what they want, and a strong local Google ranking.

## Positioning

- Complex, modern decoration: every cake is made to the customer's wish, "a work of art". The biggest cake so far had 7 tiers; another was a round cake 1 m across.
- Ingredients: Belgian chocolate, home-grown fruit, home-made sponges and fillings, everything made by hand. Slava cookies under the line "Kolači rukom pravljeni".
- Fast replies, warmth and hospitality toward customers.
- About 1,500 cakes a year, about 100 of them wedding cakes.

## Operating Context

- Ordering happens only by phone, Viber or WhatsApp (preferred, so details stay in the chat), or in person. **No online ordering, no online payment, no email contact form.**
- Phones: 064 9643302 and 064 4537545. Orders accepted 10:00 to 19:00; pick-up by arrangement. (Google Maps lists opening at 09:00; to be confirmed.)
- Workshop only, no shop floor: pick-up point with parking in front. Small cakes and slava cookies are sometimes available without pre-order.
- Lead times: ordinary cake 5 to 7 days ahead; wedding cake at least a month, subject to free dates. Rush orders only if a slot and a light weekend allow.
- Deposit 30%, cash or bank transfer; balance by arrangement. Cancellation about 10 days ahead; complex wedding cakes cannot be cancelled. Changes accepted before baking starts.
- Delivery free within Leskovac; outside the city at extra cost (surrounding places, Niš, Vranje). Large cakes are delivered on a stand and assembled on site.
- Customer gives: date and time, name, phone, delivery place, flavour, size, decoration (a photo of an example).
- Size chosen by number of people or by kilogram; minimum 2 kg, no maximum.
- Free tasting for wedding cakes by arrangement.
- Storage: fridge; cakes up to 7 days, lenten (posni) small cookies up to a month.

## Product Principles

1. Every page ends in a message or a call: Viber and WhatsApp first, phone second.
2. Show the cakes; photographs carry the sale, copy supports them.
3. Answer the questions people actually send (price per kg, how many kg for N guests, how early to order, delivery) before they have to ask.
4. Local first: Leskovac and the surrounding towns, plus the diaspora ordering for family.
5. Only true claims: real numbers from the client, no invented reviews, partners or awards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first; many visitors come from Instagram on phones. Older relatives and diaspora customers must find the phone numbers instantly. Respect reduced motion.
